# StoryCam — ASO keyword set

Google Play has **no keyword field** (unlike the App Store): ranking
comes from the title, the short description, and the full description,
plus installs and ratings. So the keywords below are placed into the
listing text in `docs/play-listing.md`, not submitted separately.

## English — by search intent

**Core (highest intent, in title + short description):**
- dual camera, dual camera video, front and back camera, record both
  cameras, front and back at once

**Reaction / social video (full description):**
- reaction video, BeReal style, picture in picture video, PiP video,
  split screen video, record yourself and the scene

**Captions (full description):**
- auto captions, automatic captions, subtitles, on-device captions,
  offline captions, karaoke captions, burn captions into video,
  Russian subtitles, English subtitles

**Creator / vlog (full description):**
- vlog, video diary, story camera, no watermark, free video recorder

**Why this set:** people search either for the capability ("record
front and back camera at once") or for the use case ("reaction video",
"BeReal style"). The caption terms are the differentiator: no other
free dual-camera recorder burns on-device multilingual captions, and
"Russian subtitles" has thin competition. "No watermark" and "free"
filter the comparison shoppers.

## Русский — by search intent

**Core:**
- двойная камера, запись с двух камер, фронтальная и задняя камера,
  видео с двух камер, снимать двумя камерами

**Reaction / social video:**
- видео реакция, реакция на видео, картинка в картинке, видео в видео,
  разделение экрана

**Captions:**
- субтитры, автосубтитры, автоматические субтитры, русские субтитры,
  субтитры на видео, субтитры на русском, субтитры офлайн

**Creator / vlog:**
- влог, видеоблог, запись видео, без водяных знаков, бесплатно

**Why this set:** Russian-language queries for dual-camera recording
and on-device subtitles have far less competition than the English
ones, and the app's Russian caption support is a real, verified
feature — this is the cheapest ranking ground StoryCam has. "Субтитры
на видео" and "автосубтитры" are the two highest-intent Russian
caption queries.

## Rules for using them

- Never stuff: each keyword appears where a human would write it.
  Play demotes keyword-stuffed listings, and reviewers read them.
- Never claim a feature the build doesn't have (no 4K, no cloud
  backup, no iOS version, no live streaming).
- Re-check the set after the first 1,000 installs: keep what converts,
  drop what doesn't. The listing text is cheap to iterate; the title
  is the most expensive word on the page — change it last.
