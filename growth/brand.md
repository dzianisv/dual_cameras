# StoryCam — brand

Defined 2026-11-21 from the shipped icon + feature graphic
(`docs/play-assets/`). Every store card is checked against this file.

- **Mark:** rounded-square "scene" lens with a sunset, overlapped by a
  circular selfie lens — two cameras at once
  (`docs/play-assets/storycam-icon-512.png`).
- **Colors:** deep navy `#111747` (background), sunset coral `#FF8A5C`
  (emphasis), white `#FFFFFF` (headlines), muted lavender `#C9CDE8`
  (sub-lines).
- **Tagline (under ten words):** "Film front & back at once."
- **Style (one adjective):** bold — dark navy, big white type, one coral
  emphasis word per headline.
- **Voice:** plain, direct, benefit-first. No jargon, no feature labels.
- **Type:** Noto Sans Bold (headlines), Noto Sans Regular (sub-lines).
