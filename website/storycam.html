What it does

Record both sides of the moment.

Your reaction and the scene, in one take, in one file — captioned before you share it.

Front + back at once

Both cameras record together into a single portrait MP4. Picture-in-picture, circle inset, or split — swap the lead camera live while recording.

Captions in Russian & English

On-device Whisper speech recognition adds burned-in captions with karaoke highlighting. Auto-detect, or force Русский or English. Silence stays silent — no captions over quiet parts.

Filters & exposure

Live filters and exposure controls while you record, so the clip looks finished before you ever open an editor.

Notifications, not pop-ups

The caption-model download and the captioning itself run as system notifications with a progress bar and a Stop button. Stop any time — your original video is always kept.

Private by design

Captions are transcribed on your phone. No account, no server, no audio upload — ever.

In sync, every time

Audio and video share one clock, so lips match across long clips. Record again right after stopping — no waiting for captions to finish.

Русские субтитры

Субтитры на русском — прямо на телефоне.

StoryCam записывает переднюю и заднюю камеры одновременно и добавляет субтитры на русском или английском — без интернета и без загрузки аудио на сервер. Распознавание речи (Whisper) работает на самом телефоне.

  • Русский и английский: автоопределение языка или принудительный выбор — «Русский» или English.
  • Караоке-подсветка: слова подсвечиваются по мере произнесения.
  • Тишина — без субтитров: паузы и шум не превращаются в слова.
  • Уведомления с кнопкой «Стоп»: загрузка модели и добавление субтитров видны в шторке уведомлений, с прогрессом и остановкой. Оригинальное видео всегда сохраняется.

Install

Three steps to your first dual video.

Download the APK

Get StoryCam 0.5.3 for Android (ARM64) from GitHub Releases. It is free, and there is no account to create.

Allow the install

Open the file and allow “install unknown apps” when Android asks. StoryCam needs camera and microphone permission — nothing else.

Record, then caption

Tap the shutter to record with both cameras. Turn captions on in the Captions sheet, pick Auto, Русский, or English — the models (~160 MB) download once, then captions run on-device.

Needs an ARM64 Android phone with concurrent-camera support (for example Pixel 6 and newer, or Galaxy S22 and newer). Without it, StoryCam falls back to single-camera recording.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is StoryCam on Google Play?

Not yet. StoryCam is currently a free Android test build that you sideload from GitHub: download the APK, allow install from unknown sources when prompted, and open it. A Google Play release is planned.

How do Russian captions work in StoryCam?

Captions run entirely on your phone with the Whisper speech model — no audio is uploaded. The first time you turn captions on, the app downloads about 160 MB of caption models. You can leave the language on Auto or force Русский or English. Single short words are the hardest case for automatic detection, so force a language for short clips.

Which phones does StoryCam work on?

StoryCam needs an ARM64 Android phone with concurrent front-and-back camera support, for example Pixel 6 and newer or Galaxy S22 and newer. If the phone cannot run both cameras at once, the app falls back to single-camera recording.

Do my videos or audio leave the phone?

No. Captions are transcribed on-device, and nothing is uploaded. The original video is always kept, and the captioned copy is saved next to it.

How do I stop a model download or captioning in StoryCam?

Both run as system notifications with a progress bar and a Stop button. Stopping a download aborts it (you can retry from the Captions sheet); stopping captioning abandons the job and keeps your original video — no captioned video is ever saved after a stop.
