StoryCam Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026 · App version 0.5.16

StoryCam records the front and back cameras together and can add on-device captions to your videos. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: sideload builds collect nothing; builds with analytics enabled send only anonymous usage events (see below).

No account, no ads

StoryCam has no account system and shows no ads. Sideload builds distributed from GitHub ship with analytics disabled: there is nothing to sign into and nothing that reports your usage back to us.

Analytics (only in builds that enable it)

Some builds include usage analytics (PostHog). Sideload builds distributed from GitHub do not have it enabled. When a build does enable it, the app sends anonymous funnel events — app opens, recordings started and finished, shares, captions finished, and caption-model download state — plus the app version and install source. It never sends video, audio, captions, file paths, or location, and it never identifies you: there is no account, no advertising ID, and no device ID. Session replay is off. Events are sent to PostHog's US servers and are used only to understand how the app is used.

Map

The live map in the corner of the screen uses OpenStreetMap. When location permission is granted, the app fetches map tiles for the area around you from tile.openstreetmap.org and sends your current coordinates to nominatim.openstreetmap.org to show the place name. That is the only place your location goes: it is sent to OpenStreetMap's servers to draw the map, it is never sent to us, and the app stores nothing about it. You can deny location permission — the app still records, and the map shows a default area instead of your position.

Your videos stay on your phone

Videos and photos you record are saved to your device's gallery. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app. Captioning — speech detection, transcription, and burning captions into the video — runs entirely on your phone.

Caption model download

On first use, the app downloads the caption models (about 160 MB) once, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any file download, this exposes your IP address to Hugging Face's servers; no personal data, account information, or video or audio content is sent. The downloaded files are verified by size and SHA-256 before use, and re-verified on every launch.

Crash reports (only in builds that enable them)

Some builds include opt-in crash reporting (Sentry). Sideload builds distributed from GitHub do not have it enabled. When a build does enable it, a crash sends: the app version, your Android version and device model, and the stack trace of the crash. It never sends video, audio, captions, or anything you recorded. Crash reports are kept by Sentry under its own retention policy and are used only to fix bugs.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera and microphone (to record), notifications (to show download and captioning progress with a Stop button), and gallery access (to save your videos). Denying notifications only means progress is shown in the app instead — recording still works.

Children

Sideload builds collect no data from anyone, including children. Builds with analytics enabled send only the anonymous usage events described above — no identity, no personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
