StoryCam Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026 · App version 0.5.3 (test release)

StoryCam records the front and back cameras together and can add on-device captions to your videos. This policy explains what the app does with your data. Short version: it doesn't collect any.

No account, no ads, no analytics

StoryCam has no account system, shows no ads, and ships no analytics or tracking SDKs. There is nothing to sign into and nothing that reports your usage back to us.

Your videos stay on your phone

Videos and photos you record are saved to your device's gallery. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app. Captioning — speech detection, transcription, and burning captions into the video — runs entirely on your phone.

Caption model download

On first use, the app downloads the caption models (about 160 MB) once, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any file download, this exposes your IP address to Hugging Face's servers; no personal data, account information, or video or audio content is sent. The downloaded files are verified by size and SHA-256 before use, and re-verified on every launch.

Crash reports (only in builds that enable them)

Some builds include opt-in crash reporting (Sentry). Test builds distributed from GitHub do not have it enabled. When a build does enable it, a crash sends: the app version, your Android version and device model, and the stack trace of the crash. It never sends video, audio, captions, or anything you recorded. Crash reports are kept by Sentry under its own retention policy and are used only to fix bugs.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera and microphone (to record), notifications (to show download and captioning progress with a Stop button), and gallery access (to save your videos). Denying notifications only means progress is shown in the app instead — recording still works.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/dzianisv/dual_cameras/issues.
